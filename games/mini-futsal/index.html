MINI FUTSAL 5v5

Five a side on a small walled pitch: a keeper and four players each. The ball bounces off the walls, so it never goes out. You are blue and attack to the right.

How to play

  • Arrows / WASD move the player with the ring and arrow. Touch the ball to dribble it.
  • Space or / shoots: tap for a short shot, hold for a hard one, then let go. Aim assist nudges shots toward the goal.
  • Space or / without the ball switches to your teammate closest to the ball. If that is already you, it is a quick lunge to win it back.
  • F or J passes to the teammate in the direction you are moving. Without the ball, F switches player.
  • Q or , swaps to the teammate behind you (closer to your own goal). E or . swaps to the teammate in front. You choose who you control, so the game never switches for you.
  • T demo (computer plays both teams), Enter pause, R restart, M sound, I info.
  • If it is a draw at full time, the next goal wins (or it stays a draw after 60 seconds).

Inspired by Freekick 3 (github.com/anttisalonen/freekick3). This is a new browser game with its own code and art, not a copy of Freekick 3.
