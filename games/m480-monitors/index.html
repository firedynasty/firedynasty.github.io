Roland M-480: monitor mixes

Two ways to build and adjust a monitor (aux) mix, with a practice board. Practice only: this page does not control a console.

Not yet checked against the M-480 manual. The steps below come from your own notes and panel photos. Steps marked UNVERIFIED (exact button order, how the LEVEL knob behaves, MTX) were flagged in those notes and still need to be confirmed. See what is left to verify.

1 · The two ways at a glance

Way 1 · Individual column

Use for: quick tweaks during a song: change one channel's send to one monitor.

Select the channel, pick the monitor, turn LEVEL. UNVERIFIED

Way 2 · Sends on Fader

Use for: setup and sound check: build or rebalance a whole monitor mix at once.

Pick the monitor, press SENDS ON FADER, and the faders become that monitor's send levels.

    Things that look like they only work one way VERIFY

    • Only Way 2, per your notes: seeing the whole monitor balance across all channels at once, and switching the CH 25–48 layer.
    • Only Way 1, per your notes: the LEVEL knob (one channel's send at a time).

    Way 1 is best for quick tweaks during a song. Way 2 is best for setup and sound check.
