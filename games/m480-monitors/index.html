Roland M-480: monitor mixes

Two ways to build and adjust a monitor (aux) mix, with a practice board. Practice only: this page does not control a console.

Checked against the Roland M-480 owner's manual (the PDF you supplied). Tags like MANUAL p. 45 show where each step was confirmed. What is not from the manual: which aux is which monitor and your channel list (those are your setup).

1 · The two ways at a glance

Way 1 · Individual column

Use for: quick tweaks during a song: change one channel's send to one monitor.

Press the channel's [SEL] button, press the [AUX] button for the monitor, turn LEVEL. MANUAL p. 24, 45

Way 2 · Sends on Fader

Use for: setup and sound check: build or rebalance a whole monitor mix at once.

Press the [AUX] button, press SENDS ON FADER (it blinks), and the faders become that monitor's send levels. MANUAL p. 45

    What the manual adds to each way

    • Way 1 only: fine adjustment (hold SHIFT while turning LEVEL) and SHIFT + [AUX] to switch that send on or off. p. 24, 45
    • Way 2 only: every channel's send for one monitor on the faders at once. If the MAIN FADER setting “SENDS ON FADER MASTER” is ticked in User Preference, the MAIN fader module becomes that aux's fader while the mode is on. p. 45, 141
    • Both: [DISP] in the AUX/MTX SENDS area opens the AUX (MTX) SENDS popup, a screen with all of a channel's sends; you do not need it to turn LEVEL. p. 24, 84
    • MTX instead of AUX: hold one of [AUX1]–[AUX8] and press the [AUX9]–[AUX16] on its right. p. 24, 45

    6 · Tips (tick them off at sound check)

    Way 1 is best for quick tweaks during a song. Way 2 is best for setup and sound check (your notes). The manual-based tips carry a page tag.

    7 · What was checked in the manual, and what is still open

    Way 1 steps
    CHECKED p. 24, 45, 84 press [AUX] to pick the destination, turn LEVEL; the channel is the one whose [SEL] is lit
    LEVEL knob
    CHECKED p. 24, 45 sets the send level to the selected AUX/MTX bus. On a stereo-linked pair, the odd-numbered aux adjusts the send pan and the even-numbered one the level
    MTX
    CHECKED p. 24 hold [AUX1]–[AUX8], press its right-hand [AUX9]–[AUX16]
    Pre / post fader
    CHECKED p. 49, 84–85 the send bar is blue for pre-EQ or pre-fader, green for post-fader, gray when the send switch is off. Set it per send in the DISP popup, or “ALL CH POINTS” for every channel at once
    Way 2 steps
    CHECKED p. 45 [AUX], then [SENDS ON FADER] (blinks while on), then the top faders
    Layers
    CHECKED p. 21 the layer buttons are [CH 1–24], [CH 25–48], [AUX/MTX] and [USER]. The aux master levels live on the [AUX/MTX] layer (AUX1–16 then MTX1–8)
    Still open
    Which aux is which monitor and the channel list come from you. Sends on Fader on the [AUX/MTX] layer, the [USER] layer and stereo-link pan are described in the manual but not practised on this board
