Horns Offense

Horns set with a 1/3 swap into a 5 ball screen. Call 1 and Call 2 differ on the weak side.

Spacing and timing here are approximate, drawn from one coach's description of the set. Your own playbook wins if it differs.
Call
Play side

Keys: ←/→ steps, Space auto-play

    Call 1 vs Call 2

    Call 1: 2 and 4 swapThe first five steps are the same. On the weak side, 2 and 4 trade spots, so 2 comes to the elbow with the ball and 4 goes to the wing.
    Call 2: 2 cuts for the layupSame first five steps. On the weak side, 2 cuts to the basket and finishes at the rim off 3's pass. 4 stays at the elbow.

    The call is made before the possession, so 4 and 2 know their job from the call. The "depends on the defense" part is still open, and 3 reads that on the drive.
