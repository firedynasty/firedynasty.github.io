How it works

Block stops almost all damage. Punches pass over a ducking fighter, kicks do not.

Landing hits fills the SP meter. At 50 you can throw a projectile.

Chain hits before the opponent recovers and each one hurts a little less. P or Enter pauses. Enter also starts a match and rematches.

Special with 50 SP throws the projectile. With less than 50 SP the same button is the spear: lunge and tackle your opponent flat on the ground. It hits hard, but you end up on the ground too, a blocked spear leaves you wide open, and you can jump over it. There is a short wait before you can spear again.

Moves buffer: press an attack a split second early and it comes out as soon as you recover.

Gamepad: D-pad or stick moves and jumps, X punch, A kick, B or a bumper blocks, Y special (spear when you have no meter). Pad 1 plays Player 1, pad 2 plays Player 2.
