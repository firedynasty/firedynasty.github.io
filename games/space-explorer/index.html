SPACE EXPLORER

Fly your ship through the Solar System. Get close to each world to scan it and learn real facts about it.

Eight planets, the Moon, the Sun and Pluto are waiting. Distances here are squeezed together so the trip is possible. In real life, the Sun to Neptune is about 4.5 billion km.

Space, ↑ or W thrust · ← → or A D turn · Shift boost · S / ↓ brake · Enter pause · T autopilot · L log · R reset · M sound. On touch, hold a finger where you want to go.

HOW TO PLAY

Goal. Scan all 11 worlds: the Sun, 8 planets, the Moon and Pluto. Each scan unlocks real facts about that world.

Scanning. Fly close to a world and stay near it for a moment. A ring fills up around it, then a fact card appears and the world is ticked off in your log.

Moves. Space, ↑ or W thrust · ← → or A D turn · S or ↓ brake · Shift with thrust to boost. On touch, hold a finger where you want to go.

Radar. The circle at the bottom right shows the whole system. The white triangle is your ship and it points the way you are facing. The yellow dot in the middle is the Sun, and planets get a green ring once scanned. A blue arrow around your ship points to the next world to scan.

Tips. The planets keep orbiting, so you may need to chase them. There is no air in space, so you keep drifting until you brake. Worlds are solid, so steer around them. Distances are squeezed together here to make the trip short.
