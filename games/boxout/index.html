Box Out Lesson

A top-down look at how a defender seals an offensive player off the rim. Walk through it, then try it yourself.

General boxing-out fundamentals, not any one coach's drill. Your own coach's terms and details win if they differ.
D = defender (you) O = offensive player S = shooter ball

Step 1 of 6

Keys: ←/→ steps, Space auto-play

    Your turn

    The shot is up. Drag D (or use the arrow keys on the court) to where you should be to box out O, then press Check. Your player is already facing the rim and in a wide base, so this one is only about where you stand.

    Drag D into position.

    Rule of thumb used here: D should be about an arm's length from O (roughly 2 to 3 ft centre-to-centre), on the straight line between O and the rim.

    Key points

    • See your man and the ball before the shot. Shout "shot!".
    • Contact first, then turn. Don't turn your back before you've found your player.
    • Stay low with a wide base and your butt against their thighs. Arms out and up.
    • Hold the seal until the ball is on its way down, then go get it with two hands.
    • Box out the player closest to you, even when the ball goes to the other side.
