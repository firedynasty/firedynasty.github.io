SHELL HOPPER

Pip the penguin has ten levels to cross. Stomp the enemies, collect coins, and throw turtle shells like grenades.

Stomping a turtle gives you a shell. Shells bounce along the ground and knock out anything in their way.

Paused

Take a breath.

How to play

  • ← → or A D run. ↑ or W jump (hold for a higher jump).
  • Space or / throws a shell in an arc (X, J, Z and Shift work too). One shell per press, so holding the key doesn't auto-throw. It bounces off walls and knocks out enemies. Pip's own shells never hurt Pip.
  • Land on enemies to stomp them. Spiky ones can only be hit with shells.
  • Hit ? blocks from below for coins, shell blocks for ammo and heart blocks for health.
  • Spikes hurt. Springs launch you. Falling into a gap costs a life.
  • Bosses guard levels 5 and 10. Stomp them or hit them with shells, then reach the flag.
