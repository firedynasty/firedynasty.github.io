About Pixel Racing ×

A retro pseudo-3D racing game, based on Jake Gordon's JavaScript Racer.

The gas is automatic. Steer with the left and right arrows (or A and D), or touch the left or right side of the screen. Hold Up or W to lift off the gas (coast). Hold Down, S or Space, or touch and hold the lap map (top-left), to brake.
